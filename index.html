How it works

Four moves
That’s the whole app

Read the full guide for the list, expiry, settings, and the written tour.

  1. 1

    Watch the hourglass

    It lives in the menu bar. The sand falls while a timer runs. Nothing else in the Dock or Cmd-Tab.

  2. 2

    Drag down

    The line grows. Quarter-hours click. :00 and :30 glow sand. Drag onto the trash if you change your mind.

  3. 3

    Release to name it

    The save window opens under the icon: duration, labels, Save. Same window as in the app.

  4. 4

    Let it run

    The menu bar counts down. When time’s up, snooze +5 or +10, or press Done.
